Destination Information

Benin City and the Benin Kingdom

Benin City, capital and largest city of Edo state, southern Nigeria. Benin City is situated on a branch of the Benin River and lies along the main highways from Lagos to the eastern states. The city is also linked by roads to Sapele, Siluko, Okene, and Ubiaja and is served by air and the Niger River delta ports of Koko and Sapele.

Source: Encyclopaedia Britannica: Benin City

Alok Ikom Stone Monoliths

Akwasnshi/Atal as the monolith is called among the Ejagham people of the Cross River State is distributed among over thirty communities. In each community, the stones are found in circles, sometimes perfect circles, facing each other standing erect, except where they have been tampered with by weather or man.

Source: UNESCO World Heritage Convention

Okomu National Park

Okomu National Park is a protected lowland rainforest national park in the Ovia South-West area of Edo State, Nigeria, about 60 kilometres northwest of Benin City. Gazetted as a forest reserve in 1935 and placed under the Nigeria National Park Service in 1999, the park preserves one of the region’s last rainforest fragments and supports rich wildlife, including African forest elephants, the white-throated guenon, roughly 150 bird species and hundreds of butterfly species. Visitors explore on guided walks along marked trails, climb a 43-metre tree house for canopy views and stay in stilted chalets near the park entrance.

Source: Tripadvisor

Ososo Hills

Ososo is a picturesque, rocky town located in Akoko-Edo LGA . Sitting at an altitude of about 1,236 feet above sea level, the Ososo landscape is a breathtaking mix of rolling granite hills, boulder formations, and temperate highland climate a rare combination in southern Nigeria. The centerpiece of this unique terrain is the majestic Oruku Rock, one of the largest and most visually striking monoliths in the area. It dominates the skyline and serves as a natural monument, spiritual marker, and a magnet for adventurers.

Source: Nigerian Heritage

Agbokim Waterfall

Enjoy Agbokim Waterfalls, popularly known as the "seven-faced wonder of nature," a breathtaking series of seven cascading streams that plunge 75 metres (246 feet) down a steep cliff into a lush tropical rainforest Akwa ibom State, Nigeria.

Source: Atlas Obscure
