Naija Trails - Website Site Plan

Iyonawan Blessing Agharese

Site Name

Naija Trails

Naija Trails is catchy, vibrant, and instantly relatable. It conveys a journey of discovery across Nigeria's rich landscapes, historical landmarks, and cultural heritage, with a dedicated spotlight on tourist attractions in Edo State.

Naija Trails

Site Purpose

The purpose of Naija Trails is to help people discover interesting tourist destinations in Edo State and other parts of Nigeria. The website will provide useful information about each destination, including its history, attractions, activities, and location. Visitors will be able to browse destinations, view photographs, learn interesting facts, and use interactive features to explore different places. The website will also include a form that allows visitors to recommend a destination they think should be featured. The site is intended for tourists, residents, students, travelers, and anyone interested in learning more about places to visit in Nigeria.

Scenarios

  • Scenario 1: A tourist planning a trip to Nigeria wants to explore Edo State's attractions. They visit Naija Trails to find information about destinations, activities, and accommodations.
  • Scenario 2: A local resident is looking for new places to visit in their own state. They use Naija Trails to discover hidden gems and learn about the history of various sites.
  • Scenario 3: A student working on a school project about Nigerian culture and tourism uses Naija Trails as a resource for research and gathering information.

Color Palette

The palette utilizes rich forest green representing Nigeria's lush natural beauty and parks, paired with warm terracotta orange symbolizing cultural heritage and earthworks, grounded by light, clean neutrals for maximum readability.

Typography

Primary Heading Font: Montserrat (Sans-serif) — Bold and clean font used for section titles, headers, and brand elements.

Body Font: Open Sans (Sans-serif) — Clean, modern font providing comfortable legibility for guides, articles, and descriptions across mobile and desktop.
